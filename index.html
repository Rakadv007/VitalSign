Feature Scaling (No Leakage)

All continuous numerical columns (num_lab_procedures, num_medications, num_procedures, etc.) are standardized using StandardScaler (\(z = \frac{x - \mu}{\sigma}\)) fitted strictly on the training partition to prevent test data leakage.

Categorical & Binary Encoding

High-cardinality diagnoses (ICD-9 codes) and medical specialties are grouped into top-10 frequent categories + "Other". Target variable readmitted is formatted as binary 2-class (0: NO, 1: Readmitted).
